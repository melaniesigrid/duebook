Questions

The things owners ask first.

Can we import our existing customers?

Yes. Bring in a spreadsheet export from your current system — names, contact details, last service dates — and Duebook builds the due list from it. You can export everything back out at any time.

Can we set different reminder intervals by service type?

Yes. Each service type gets its own cycle — a six-month oil change and an annual inspection can live on the same customer with separate due dates.

Does it send emails automatically?

No — and that's on purpose. Duebook drafts the reminder; your team reviews it and hits send. Nothing goes to a customer without a person approving it.

Can staff review reminders before sending?

Always. Every reminder starts as a draft. Staff can edit the wording, snooze it, or skip it entirely. The due list tracks what's been reviewed and what's still waiting.

Can it track renewals and maintenance due dates?

Yes — both. Renewals track contract or membership end dates; maintenance tracks intervals from the last service. Each shows up in the same due list with its own reason.

Does it replace our CRM?

It doesn't try to. Duebook does one job: it tells you who is due, why they're due, and what reminder to send. If you already run a CRM, Duebook sits beside it and owns the reminder loop.
